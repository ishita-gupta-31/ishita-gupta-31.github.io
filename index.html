Hi! 👋

I'm Ishita, a human-AI interaction researcher currently working as a Research Associate at the Melange Lab, IIIT Delhi, with Dr. Pushpendra Singh. My current work explores how people interact with AI in mental health contexts, specifically how AI systems can be designed to support users with OCD safely and responsibly. In Lab, I have also worked on understanding how aptly frontier LLMs are able to reflect and understand cultural context in Indian maternal health scenarios.


More broadly, I'm interested in responsible AI and human-centred explainable AI for healthcare. In my future research, I wish to explore how we can make AI systems that are not just accurate, but explainable, fair, and genuinely useful to the people who need them most.


Before this, I worked as an AI Safety Researcher at Enkrypt AI, where I focused on representational harms and bias in large language models, developing evaluation frameworks for auditing socioeconomic, gender, and partisan biases across global and Indian contexts. Two of these works were accepted as first-author workshop posters to Neurips 2025 and AAAI 2026. My work on gender bias in Hindi text generation was published as a first-author paper at ACM FAccT 2025, in collaboration with Prof. Tapan Parikh at Cornell Tech.

To know more about my work, check out my resume or drop me an email!
